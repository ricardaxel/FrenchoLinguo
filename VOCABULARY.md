# FrenchoLinguo — Vocabulary plan (A1, draft v0.1)

Companion to [CURRICULUM.md](CURRICULUM.md). The grammar lessons (L1–L35) are the **spine**. The vocabulary themes **hang off it**: each theme unlocks when the grammar needed to *use* its words has been taught.

---

## 1. Principles

1. **Words come with a sentence frame.** A word is never taught alone. Each theme pack ships with 2–3 frames built only from grammar the learner already knows (e.g. Animals after L8: *Il y a un chat dans le jardin.* "There is a cat in the garden.").
2. **Nouns come with their article.** A card shows *la chaise*, never *chaise*. When the article hides the gender (*l'*, *les*), the card adds a badge: *l'arbre (m.)*, *l'eau (f.)*. Gender is tested in every pack (pick *le* or *la*).
3. **Pre-teach, then use.** A theme pack is placed just *before* the grammar lesson that will use its words. The lesson's examples then feel familiar, and the learner's attention goes to the rule rather than to new words.
4. **Spiral.** Each theme comes back in 2–3 later lessons with a new grammar point (food: *il y a* → partitive → *je voudrais* → passé composé). Coming back is a better use of a session than adding more words.
5. **Core vs. elective.** *Core* themes are needed by the grammar lessons, and every learner gets them. *Elective* themes (animals, clothes, hobbies…) are chosen by the learner, for motivation and personal relevance.
6. **Frequency first.** Inside a theme, the most frequent words go into pack 1, using Lexique's subtitle frequency (`freqfilms2`, see [RESEARCH.md](RESEARCH.md)).
7. **Each word is taught once.** A word introduced in a lesson or an earlier pack is never a pack word again (L1–L2 already teach *bonjour, merci, le livre, l'eau, le pain, la table, le chat, l'école…*). It comes back in frames instead. The validator flags duplicates.
8. **Spelling and sound are part of the word card.** Each word stores its gender, irregular plural or feminine, and its pronunciation from Lexique (silent letters, an *h aspiré* that blocks elision: *le héros*, not ~~l'héros~~). See §5.
9. **Cognates are a gift, false friends a trap.** English shares thousands of words with French. Cognates speed up early packs; false friends (*librairie* "bookshop", *actuellement* "currently") get a warning card.

### Sizes
- **Pack** = 8 words, learned in a single 3-minute **Words** session (see/hear → recognise → recall, with the article).
- **Theme** = 2–3 packs spread over the course (core pack early, extension packs later).
- A1 target: about **650 words** in total = about 250 words introduced inside grammar lessons (function words, core verbs) + about 400 in theme packs (≈ 50 packs).

## 2. Session mix

Session type **Words** (3 min), as in TurkoLinguo. A typical day might look like this:

| Day type | Sessions |
|---|---|
| Grammar day | Learn (L*n*) → Review |
| Practice day | Practise (L*n*) → Words (pack) → Review |
| Short day (3 min) | Review only, mixing vocabulary + grammar cards |

Rule of thumb: **about 1 Words session per grammar session**. A Words pack only becomes available when the learner has done the grammar lesson it depends on (the "unlock" column below).

## 4. Word lists (core pack + extension)

Flags: `[m]` / `[f]` gender (always stored; written here only when the article hides it), `[pl:…]` irregular plural, `[f:…]` irregular feminine (adjectives, nationalities, jobs), `[h]` *h aspiré* (no elision or liaison), `[inv]` invariable, `[ff]` false friend (gets a warning card), `[phrase]` = set phrase, shown unsplit and never used to build new forms. ## 5. Word-card data (needed by the form engine)

French morphology is small, but the engine must never produce ~~les chevals~~, ~~la eau~~ or ~~l'héros~~. Most of this data comes from Lexique (gender, number, phonetics) and Wiktionary (irregular plurals, feminines); the A1 list (about 650 words) is then hand-checked.

| Field / flag | Meaning | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| `gender` (required on nouns) | m / f / both | *la table (f)*, *l'enfant (m/f)*, *le / la journaliste* |
| `pl` | irregular plural (default: +s) | *-eau → -eaux* (*le bateau*), *-al → -aux* (*le cheval*), *-ail → -aux* (*le travail*); exceptions *le festival → les festivals*; *l'œil → les yeux*; *-s/-x/-z* unchanged (*le bras, le prix, le nez*) |
| `f` | irregular feminine (adjectives, nationalities, jobs; default: +e) | *-en → -enne* (*italienne*), *-er → -ère* (*cher → chère*), *-eux → -euse*, *-f → -ve* (*neuf → neuve*), *blanc → blanche*, *long → longue*, *beau / bel / belle*, *vieux / vieil / vieille*, *nouveau / nouvel / nouvelle* |
| `inv` | adjective doesn't agree | *orange, marron, bon marché, sympa* |
| `h` (*h aspiré*) | no elision, no liaison | *le héros, le hamburger, la honte, les haricots* (no liaison). Mute h (default): *l'hôtel, l'heure* |
| `phon` | IPA from Lexique, for silent-letter and liaison display | *grand* /ɡʁɑ̃/ (liaison [t]: *grand‿homme*), *les* /le/ (liaison [z]) |
| `ff` | false friend; shows a warning card | *la librairie, actuellement, sensible, la monnaie, rester* |
| `phrase` | set phrase: unsplit, not a source for generated forms | *s'il vous plaît, je voudrais, ça va* |

Verbs store their **conjugation class** instead (`er`, `er-cer`, `er-ger`, `er-e`/`er-é` stem change, `er-ll`/`er-tt`, `ir-iss`, `re`, or an `irr` table) and their **auxiliary** in the passé composé (`avoir` / `être`; reflexives always `être`).

## 6. Media decisions (proposed, to confirm)

### Images → open licence (same rules as TurkoLinguo)
- **Sources:** Wikimedia Commons and Openverse, filtered to **CC0 / CC BY / CC BY-SA** (no NC, no ND). **OpenMoji** (CC BY-SA 4.0) as the fallback for abstract words and a consistent icon style. TurkoLinguo's own icons can be reused where the object is the same.
- **Every asset stores** `source_url, author, license, license_url`, and the app's **Credits** screen is generated from them.

### Audio → native recordings
- **Words: Lingua Libre.** French is one of its largest languages. **To do:** run TurkoLinguo's `check_audio_coverage.py` (adapted to `Category:Lingua_Libre_pronunciation-fra`) on the A1 list and record the result in `data/audio_coverage.tsv`. Prefer recordings by speakers from France, to match the target variety.
- **Sentences:** Tatoeba has many more French sentences with audio than Turkish ones; filter by per-speaker licence (no NC). Lesson sentences and dialogues not covered → 1–2 native speakers with a written CC BY or work-for-hire agreement.
- **TTS (Piper / the phone's `fr-FR` voice)** is acceptable for ordinary words without a recording. **Native recordings are required** for:
  - the L1–L2 sound contrasts (*le / les, tu / tout, vin / vent / vont*);
  - liaison and elision examples (*les‿amis* vs *les haricots*);
  - T1 greetings and T1b survival phrases;
  - "singular or plural by ear" items (*il parle / ils parlent* must sound identical, *il a / ils‿ont* must not);
  - question intonation (*Tu es français ?* vs the statement).
